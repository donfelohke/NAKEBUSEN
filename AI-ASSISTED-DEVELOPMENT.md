# AI Assistance, Customer Data and Source Provenance

**Status:** Proposed business and repository policy  
**Canonical policy:** [FELOH Corpus — AI-Assisted Development and Source Integrity](https://github.com/donfelohke/feloh-corpus/blob/main/AI-ASSISTED-DEVELOPMENT.md)

This repository applies the canonical policy to NAKEBUSEN's business operations, public copy, service catalogue, website-related documentation and digital-service work.

## Rules

- Never place customer names, phone numbers, photos, private messages, appointment records, payment references, credentials or other customer-confidential information into public or third-party AI tools.
- Use synthetic or properly anonymised examples when exploring workflows, copy or software.
- AI-generated testimonials, customer histories, qualifications, reviews, before/after claims and service outcomes must never be presented as real.
- A human maintainer approves public prices, service scope, contact routes, payment instructions and operational claims.
- Disclose material AI assistance in substantive public-facing or technical contributions where it affects authorship, rights or factual reliability.
- Verify factual claims and citations independently. Model output is not evidence.
- Preserve third-party licences, attribution, image rights and consent. The Gallery remains a separately governed asset repository.
- Never use an LLM to rewrite unauthorised source code or assets to disguise their origin.

## Business boundary

NAKEBUSEN remains the only commercial transaction interface in the ecosystem. AI assistance does not approve a booking, verify a payment, create customer consent or authorise publication. Enquiry is not booking; payment verification must follow an explicitly approved operational process.

## Licence boundary

The existing GPL-3.0 repository licence is unchanged. It applies only to material actually covered by its grant; it does not automatically license customer content, third-party assets, photographs, logos or other separately owned material.

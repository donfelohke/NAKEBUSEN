# NAKEBUSEN Governance

## Purpose

NAKEBUSEN grounds the ecosystem in beauty craft, loc care, practical digital services, customer relationships, dignity and livelihood.

## Public service pathways

NAKEBUSEN presents two public service practices: **LOCS** and **DIGITAL**. Customer-facing offers, enquiries, quotes, appointment details and service confirmations should be handled through the relevant approved NAKEBUSEN pathway. WARENA powers the technical practice and maintains technical knowledge and implementation records.

This is the intended operating model, not a claim that any booking, payment or customer-management system has been implemented. A public enquiry channel does not itself confirm an appointment or a completed job.

## Public claims

Publish only approved, current information. Do not invent customer testimonials, credentials, qualifications, prices, availability, service outcomes or guarantees. Treat the service catalogue as a reviewable source and check public copies for drift.

For LOCS, distinguish consultation, retouch/styling, Sisterlocs maintenance, artificial loc installation and any other service by scope. For DIGITAL, define the problem, assumptions, deliverables, risks, tests and handover before claiming completion.

## Evidence and status

Mark proposals as proposals, research as research, and operational claims according to the evidence available. A written procedure is not proof it has been executed. A technical specification is not proof of deployment. A CI result does not prove real-world behaviour unless the tested environment and scope support that conclusion.

## Customer privacy

Do not place customer records, private conversations, photos, contact details beyond approved public business contacts, appointment history, payment references or credentials in Git. Collect only information necessary for a defined purpose and keep it in an appropriately controlled system.

## Stewardship

Use focused branches and pull requests for material changes. Document decisions and handover procedures so the business can be stewarded by contributors without depending on permanent founder control.

This file states intended governance; it does not claim that booking, payment, privacy controls, or a customer management system is implemented.

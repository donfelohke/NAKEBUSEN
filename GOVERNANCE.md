# NAKEBUSEN Governance

## Purpose

NAKEBUSEN grounds the ecosystem in beauty craft, loc care, customer relationships, dignity, and livelihood.

## Transaction boundary

**ONLY NAKEBUSEN TRANSACTS.** Other ecosystem domains may support creative or technical work, but transaction-related commitments must be made through NAKEBUSEN's explicitly approved operating process.

## Public claims

Publish only approved, current information. Do not invent customer testimonials, credentials, qualifications, prices, availability, service outcomes, or guarantees. Treat the service catalogue as a reviewable source and check public copies for drift.

## Customer privacy

Do not place customer records, private conversations, photos, contact details, appointment history, payment references, or credentials in Git. Collect only information necessary for a defined purpose and keep it in an appropriately controlled system.

## Stewardship

Use focused branches and pull requests for material changes. Document decisions and handover procedures so the business can be stewarded by contributors without depending on permanent founder control.

This file states intended governance; it does not claim that booking, payment, privacy controls, or a customer management system is implemented.

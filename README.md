## Public information and verification

Service names, prices, locations, availability, contact details, durations, materials and timing can change. Publish only information explicitly approved for current use. Do not invent testimonials, qualifications, certifications, service guarantees, outcomes or availability.

The catalogue currently marks some prices and service details as requiring current owner confirmation. Website prices such as KES 700 for Retouch & Styling, KES 1,500 for Sisterlocs Retie and KES 3,500 for Artificial Locs Install should not be treated as freshly reconfirmed by this repository change.

NAKEBUSEN DIGITAL is the public technology-services practice powered by WARENA. The DIGITAL playbook defines a proposed delivery method; it does not prove that every listed service is currently available, priced, or already deployed.

## Privacy

Do not commit customer names, phone numbers beyond the published business contact, appointment histories, client photos, private messages, payment references, credentials, private network details that create risk, or other personal records to Git. Keep necessary operational records in an appropriately controlled system.

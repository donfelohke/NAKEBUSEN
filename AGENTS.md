# NAKEBUSEN contributor and agent instructions

## Governing boundary
**ONLY NAKEBUSEN TRANSACTS.** Read `README.md`, `GOVERNANCE.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `SECURITY.md`, the service catalogue, and the [NAKEBUSEN Operator skill](https://github.com/donfelohke/feloh-corpus/blob/main/skills/nakebusen-operator/SKILL.md) before changing business-facing material.

## Business and customer safeguards
- Treat the approved service catalogue as the source of truth for service names and prices; check all public copies for drift.
- Never invent availability, qualifications, testimonials, outcomes, guarantees, customer identities, or booking/payment functionality.
- Enquiries are not confirmed bookings or payments. Preserve manual confirmation unless a real approved process proves otherwise.
- Require explicit human approval before changing prices, services, contact destinations, business terms, customer-facing claims, or publication policy.
- Never commit customer records, private conversations, photos without documented consent, appointment history, payment references, credentials, or private supplier/staff data.

## Change control
- Inspect existing copy and implementation before editing.
- Use focused branches and pull requests for material changes.
- Record evidence for published claims and label unknowns honestly.
- Do not claim that a transaction, booking, payment, or deployment occurred unless the relevant system confirms it.

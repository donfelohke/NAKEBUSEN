## Scope and method

This scan uses providers' public websites surfaced during research on 2026-10-26. Prices and claims below are transcribed as public claims, not independently confirmed quotations. A website can be outdated or internally inconsistent; confirm the exact service, inclusions, and current price directly before relying on it. Do not assume similarly named services are technically equivalent.

No attempt is made to infer turnover, demand, profitability, customer retention or true market share from public web pages or review counts.

## Important price-comparison caveat

Public prices are not directly comparable until service scope is aligned. “Dreadlock retouch,” “Sisterlocks retightening,” “microlocs maintenance,” “installation,” and “artificial loc installation” can require materially different time, technique, materials and expertise.

NAKEBUSEN's currently owner-supplied featured starting prices (still requiring current owner confirmation before publication) are:
- Retouch & Styling — KES 700.
- Sisterlocs Retie — KES 1,500.
- Artificial Locs Install — KES 3,500.

These are not a like-for-like comparison with Sisterlocks installation prices. Keep the distinction explicit. Do not claim NAKEBUSEN is cheaper, better, or the best value based only on this scan.

## Initial UX findings

### Patterns worth testing
1. **Put service names and starting prices together.** Avoid making visitors navigate multiple pages just to discover whether a service is offered.
2. **Explain what changes the final quote.** State that hair length, density, loc condition, materials and scope may affect the final cost, where applicable.
3. **Separate enquiry from confirmed booking.** A contact link or form submission is not proof that a slot is reserved.
4. **Make location and contact visible.** Distinguish Eldoret practice from Lodwar roots; provide a working WhatsApp action and an understandable location description.
5. **Use authentic portfolio evidence.** Publish only approved work images; obtain client permission and avoid exposing names, phone numbers or private conversations.
6. **Describe service exclusions.** NAKEBUSEN does not offer braiding; make that boundary clear without disparaging other providers.
7. **Make aftercare practical.** Provide appropriate maintenance and aftercare guidance, with clear limits and no unsupported guarantees.
8. **Design for mobile.** Check the page at 320, 360, 390, 430 and 760 CSS-pixel widths; verify price readability, tap targets, WhatsApp links, language toggle, theme toggle and no horizontal overflow.

## Follow-up fieldwork checklist

Before treating this as a competitor matrix, for each provider:
- Open the current official page and record the date.
- Confirm the exact service and what is included.
- Ask whether the listed price is current and whether a consultation is needed.
- Confirm location, hours and booking method.
- Review mobile behaviour and accessibility.
- Distinguish first-party claims from third-party directory data and customer reviews.
- Record contradictory evidence rather than selecting the most convenient figure.
- Keep the notes factual and respectful; do not copy site text or visual identity.

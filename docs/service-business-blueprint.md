## Purpose

Make the current loc-care service easy to understand, enquire about and deliver consistently. Keep the future physical salon as a later investment decision; do not imply a salon premises, staffing or booking platform exists unless separately verified.

## Current public service baseline

The existing public website and repository README list these starting prices. Confirm before each release:

| Service | Starting price |
|---|---:|
| Retouch & Styling | KES 700 |
| Sisterlocs Retie | KES 1,500 |

These are starting prices, not unconditional quotes. Final cost depends on the client's locs, requested work, materials and any additional service agreed in advance. **Do not advertise braiding** unless the owner explicitly approves a later change.

## Ideal initial customer

People seeking loc maintenance or styling who value clear communication, careful craft, respectful treatment and transparent prices. Avoid unsupported promises about results, durability, qualifications or availability.

## Enquiry-to-service workflow

1. Receive an enquiry through the approved contact channel.
2. Identify the requested service, relevant loc-care needs, preferred timing and any home-visit request.
3. Explain the starting price and any information needed to quote accurately.
4. Confirm availability, final price, scope, location and any travel or material cost before the customer commits.
5. Deliver the agreed service and explain relevant aftercare where appropriate.
6. Record only the operational information needed for follow-up; keep private customer records out of this public repository.
7. Invite feedback without inventing or publishing a testimonial without permission.

An enquiry is not a confirmed appointment; a website is not a booking engine or payment processor.

## Service standard

- Communicate price and scope before work begins.
- Obtain consent before any additional work, photography or publication.
- Explain delays or changes clearly.
- Do not pressure customers to purchase add-ons.
- Keep customer details, photos, messages, appointment history and payment references out of Git.
- Keep public claims accurate and dated where information changes.

## Communications

**Core promise:** “Beautiful locs. Careful craft. Clear prices.”  
**Brand line:** “Your Beauty. Your Way.”  
**Supporting line:** “Your Crown. Your Craft.”

Use plain, welcoming language. Tell visitors what is available, the starting price, what affects the final quote and how to enquire. Do not use internal ecosystem terminology as a substitute for service details.

## Business records and measures

Track through an appropriately controlled private system, not this public repository:
- Enquiries by service and source.
- Confirmed appointments and completed services.
- Repeat bookings and customer feedback.
- Revenue, materials, travel costs and service time.
- Cancellations, rescheduling and recurring operational issues.

Use these observations to improve service quality and estimate demand. Do not claim validated market demand until records support it.

## Future salon decision gate

A physical salon is a future option, not an assumed current capability. Before committing, assess repeat demand, location, rent and deposit, utilities, equipment, licensing obligations, staffing, cash buffer, expected service capacity and contribution margin. Document the decision and assumptions before making a long-term commitment.

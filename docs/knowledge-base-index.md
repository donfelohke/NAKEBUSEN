## Shared rules

1. Approved public service facts belong in the service catalogue and must be reviewed before publishing.
2. Operational methods belong in domain procedures; technical implementation evidence belongs in the relevant source repository.
3. Mark every important statement as a verified fact, a source-reported statement, a recommendation, a proposal, or an unknown where ambiguity matters.
4. An unconfirmed price, duration, location, service scope or availability is not a public promise.
5. Do not store customer identities, appointment histories, private messages, payment references, passwords, or other sensitive records in this public repository.
6. Link to canonical documents instead of maintaining conflicting copies.
7. Keep concepts and proposed procedures distinct from observed or tested operations.

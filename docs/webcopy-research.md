**Status:** RESEARCH / DOCUMENTATION · 25 October 2026

**Copy rule:** Route offers, enquiries and service confirmations through the relevant NAKEBUSEN LOCS or DIGITAL pathway. An enquiry is not a booking.

This note summarises market evidence and copy recommendations. It does not change live prices until the owner confirms.

---

## 1. What strong Kenya loc sites do

Observed patterns (Nairobi/Nakuru/market guides, 2025–2026):

| Pattern | Why it converts |
|---------|------------------|
| **Starting prices in KES** | Reduces “how much?” friction; WhatsApp still closes the quote |
| **“From …” + what changes the price** | Length, density, materials, time |
| **Duration where known** | Sets expectations (e.g. retouch 2–3 hrs elsewhere) |
| **WhatsApp as primary CTA** | Kenya default booking channel |
| **Salon · Home · Call** | Matches mobile / estate demand |
| **M-Pesa named** | Payment clarity |
| **No fake reviews / invented certs** | Trust; matches FELOH evidence discipline |

Example market anchors (competitors — not NAKEBUSEN prices):

- Retouch often **KES 800–2,500+** in urban listings  
- Artificial / temporary install often **KES 3,500–6,500+** by length  
- Sisterlocks install (full) often much higher in Nairobi (multi-session); **retie/maintenance** is the comparable line to NAKEBUSEN’s “Sisterlocs 1,500”  

NAKEBUSEN published starts (**700 / 1,500 / 3,500**) sit at the **accessible / regional** end of national ranges. Keep “From” language; do not claim “cheapest in Kenya.”

## 5. Brand-safe voice (from constitution)

- **Grace:** dignity, crown, craft, care — not “glam transformation” hype  
- **Grit:** practical systems language; no fake AI product claims  
- **Glory:** creative practice; no unverified awards  
- Customer-facing offers and confirmations use the relevant NAKEBUSEN service pathway.  
- Prefer plain English; short Sheng/Swahili only where natural on socials  

Avoid: testimonial carousels without real consent; “best in Kenya”; clinical claims; IKAI as product.

---

## 6. Site structure checklist (webcopy)

1. One clear hero promise (craft + systems or crown-first for beauty ads).  
2. Service menu with **From KES** + disclaimer.  
3. WhatsApp CTA above the fold on mobile.  
4. Dual location if both are true.  
5. Social proof only if real (or omit).  
6. Footer: X · Instagram · TikTok · Facebook business · GitHub.  
7. Schema `sameAs` matches live accounts.

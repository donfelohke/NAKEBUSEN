## Purpose and positioning

NAKEBUSEN exists to cultivate beauty, dignity, practical capability and self-determination through craft, culture, community and technology.

It is a Kenyan enterprise with a history beginning in Kanamkemer, Lodwar, Turkana County, and a continuing loc-care practice. Public operating location, service area, contact details, prices and availability must always be checked against current approved operations records.

**Positioning statement:** For people seeking thoughtful loc care and practical, clearly scoped digital assistance, NAKEBUSEN is a heritage-conscious Kenyan enterprise that combines professional craft with a wider commitment to human agency and understandable technology. WARENA contributes technical capability without replacing NAKEBUSEN as the customer-facing enterprise.

## Master story

NAKEBUSEN begins with people: identity, appearance, dignity and the relationships built through careful service. Its loc-care practice treats craft as a discipline and beauty as an individual expression rather than a single imposed standard.

Its story is rooted in Lodwar, Turkana County, and continues through practical enterprise. The work is grounded in listening, explaining the service, agreeing scope and price, and respecting the person.

WARENA extends the enterprise's interest in capability into technology: libre-first software, local-first systems, GNU/Linux, infrastructure and engineering that can be inspected and maintained. These are commitments and areas of work; each public capability claim must match verified implementation.

GENJE ZA WARENA expresses the cultural and creative dimension through sound, rhythm, movement, livecoding and computational experimentation. The shared ethos is GRACE · GRIT · GLORY.

**Beautiful Roots. Sovereign Systems.**

## Brand promise and service behaviour

- Respect the client and their individuality.
- Explain the service, scope, starting price and relevant conditions before agreement.
- Be clear when a final price depends on hair length, condition, time or requirements.
- Do not imply that an enquiry confirms a booking.
- Obtain consent before capturing or publishing identifiable client images or testimonials.
- Keep service records and private customer information confidential.
- Do not advertise braiding or any service absent from the approved catalogue.
- Never invent availability, qualifications, testimonials, discounts, guarantees or outcomes.

## Brand voice

Warm, assured, respectful, specific and direct. Use plain English and natural Kenyan English; use Kiswahili/Sheng where it serves the audience without forced slang. Lead with the client's need and the service benefit, not abstract philosophy. Keep cultural claims attributed and technically precise.

## Customer-facing message hierarchy

1. NAKEBUSEN.
2. The actual service or customer benefit.
3. Approved starting price and conditions.
4. Clear enquiry/contact route.
5. WARENA POWERED or GENJE ZA WARENA only where contextually relevant.
6. GRACE · GRIT · GLORY and master tagline as supporting philosophy.

For loc-care campaigns, service earns the enquiry; imagery earns attention; cultural identity makes the campaign memorable. Do not let GENJE motion or technical slogans obscure the actual loc-care offer.

## Commercial fact controls

Current service names, starting prices, contact details, locations, payment instructions and booking terms are controlled by the service catalogue and operations documents. Before publication, check those records rather than copying historic campaign drafts. The approved public loc-care baseline has included Retouch & Styling — KES 700+, Sisterlocs Retie — KES 1,500+, and Artificial Locs Installation — KES 3,500; confirm the current catalogue before reuse. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking.

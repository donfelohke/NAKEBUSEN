## Design copy and visual direction

Use the shared editorial African-rooted design system: near-black, ivory, forest green, sage, ochre/gold and terracotta; Cormorant Garamond display, Inter body and JetBrains Mono metadata. Keep loc care and the human customer experience primary. Use craft details, clean parting evidence and documentary imagery only when rights and consent are recorded.

Avoid generic salon/luxury templates, beauty scoring, default Rasta shorthand, generic AI imagery, decorative cultural claims and overpromising.

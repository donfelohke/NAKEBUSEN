## Publication gate

Before publishing a poster, verify:
- correct business and service hierarchy;
- exact service names, prices and starting-price notation;
- current contact details and location;
- approved logo and brand palette;
- legible typography and CTA at intended display size;
- source-image provenance and subject consent;
- accurate quotation, historical, cultural and technical claims;
- documented owner approval.

## BLACK EXCELLENCE · GRACE

NAKEBUSEN expresses **GRACE**: beauty, heritage, dignity, care, craft and grounded enterprise. The wider philosophy is **BLACK EXCELLENCE — GRACE · GRIT · GLORY**. GRACE is not merely decorative beauty; it is care, skill, respect and human value in practice.

## Why NAKEBUSEN?

NAKEBUSEN is the founder-chosen Turkana-rooted name associated with **“beautiful.”** This is the founder-provided naming context and the brand's adopted meaning. Related Turkana beauty vocabulary has been recorded in research, but the exact commercial spelling, pronunciation and morphology still require confirmation by fluent Turkana speakers. Keep that qualification visible; do not overstate etymology.

NAKEBUSEN began in **Kanamkemer, Lodwar, Turkana County**. This is enterprise history. The current loc-care operating address remains **Eldoret Town, Uasin Gishu County, Kenya**. Never substitute the historical origin for the current service location or vice versa.

## NAKEBUSEN LOCS · #BLACKPEARL

Locs are the specialist beauty-industry focus because they unite natural-hair care, maintenance craft, styling skill, identity, personal expression and continuing client relationships. The positioning should prioritise professional loc care rather than a generic, interchangeable salon claim.

**#BLACKPEARL** is a subordinate editorial/campaign identity for NAKEBUSEN LOCS—not a separate business, checkout or universal beauty ideal. It should celebrate varied Black beauty and loc expression without prescribing one appearance.

Approved public loc-care prices remain fixed and controlled by [service-catalogue.md](service-catalogue.md):
- Retouch & Styling — KES 700
- Sisterlocs Retie — KES 1,500
- Artificial Locs Installation — KES 3,500

Do not add “+” or “from”. Do not advertise braiding or shaving. Do not invent availability, discounts, testimonials or guarantees. An enquiry is not a booking.

## NAKEBUSEN DIGITAL · POWERED BY WARENA

NAKEBUSEN DIGITAL is the customer-facing direction for practical technology, computing and digital services. WARENA supplies the engineering capability; it does not replace NAKEBUSEN as the customer relationship and transaction point. Candidate service families (GNU/Linux, computer care, networking, local-first systems and technical integration) are not automatically live offers. Each engagement needs a feasibility check and explicit scope, price, timing, deliverables and support agreement.

**ONLY NAKEBUSEN TRANSACTS.** IKAI remains research / work in progress; GENJE remains creative practice; WARENA is technical capability, not a separate checkout.

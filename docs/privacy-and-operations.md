# NAKEBUSEN Privacy and Operations

## Public versus private information

Public repository content may include approved brand assets, service descriptions, price lists, non-sensitive procedures, and public contact channels.

Keep customer identities, phone numbers beyond the published business contact, appointment details, photos, private messages, payment records, and staff/supplier personal data outside Git in an appropriately controlled system.

## Enquiry workflow

1. Publish the approved service catalogue and contact channel.
2. Receive an enquiry through the stated channel.
3. Clarify service requirements and quote only from current approved information.
4. Confirm appointment details directly through the approved operating process.
5. Record only the minimum information needed to fulfil the service.
6. Handle any payment only through the explicitly approved transaction process.
7. Retain or delete operational records according to a documented purpose and applicable requirements.

An enquiry is not a confirmed booking. A quotation is not proof of payment. This repository does not itself implement these steps or provide a booking/payment processor.

## Operational handover

Document the responsible role, current service source, contact-channel ownership, cancellation/rescheduling process, record access, incident handling, and continuity plan. Do not publish private operating credentials.

# Similar Projects and Benchmark Research

**Status:** Initial research plan; not a completed market audit  
**Prepared:** 2026-10-26  
**Scope:** NAKEBUSEN Locs • Digital, powered by WARENA; IKAI reflective-compute research; GENJE / creative computation  
**Audience:** Public-facing website, business planning, technical documentation and knowledge-base contributors

## Purpose

Identify projects worth studying because they provide comparable practices, not because they are assumed to be direct competitors. Keep commercial benchmarks, technical references and cultural/creative references distinct. Use evidence to inform decisions; do not copy another project's identity or imply endorsement or affiliation.

## Evidence labels

- **Verified reference:** a primary project site or official documentation exists at the linked URL.
- **Benchmark hypothesis:** a practice worth investigating; effectiveness has not yet been established for NAKEBUSEN.
- **Needs field research:** local prices, customer sentiment, operating status, service availability, traffic, revenue and conversion rates must be independently checked.
- **Recommendation:** a proposed action, not a claim that it has been implemented.

Do not infer revenue, market share, service quality or customer satisfaction from a website, repository popularity or social-media follower count alone.

## Research tracks

### A. NAKEBUSEN Locs — commercial and customer-experience benchmarks

Primary market: Eldoret, Uasin Gishu. Lodwar / Kanamkemer is the heritage and origin context.

For each relevant provider, record:
- Provider name and official public URL.
- Date checked and evidence capture (public page, price list or direct confirmation).
- Services offered and excluded services.
- Published starting price, currency, what is included and any conditions.
- Location, service area, opening hours and booking/contact route.
- Consultation requirements, maintenance/aftercare information and portfolio evidence.
- Accessibility, mobile usability, language, clarity and time-to-enquiry.
- Review themes only when sourced from identifiable public review pages; distinguish anecdote from representative evidence.

Do not invent competitor prices or use old screenshots as current evidence. Do not scrape or store private client data. Do not publish unsupported claims such as “best”, “number one”, guaranteed results, qualifications or customer testimonials.

**Current NAKEBUSEN positioning to preserve:** loc-care first; clear service scope; enquiries are not confirmed bookings; no braiding; current featured starting prices and availability require owner confirmation before publication. Public copy should distinguish Eldoret practice from Lodwar roots.

### B. WARENA — independent digital services and systems engineering

Benchmark service providers and open-source projects across:
- GNU/Linux setup and administration.
- Router, LAN, Wi-Fi, VLAN and connectivity deployment.
- Backup, recovery, migration and maintenance.
- Local-first and offline-capable systems.
- Reproducible development environments and documented handover.
- Scope, exclusions, prerequisites, testing, rollback and support boundaries.

For every proposed service, distinguish **available now**, **pilot / experimental**, **planned**, and **not offered**. Only publish an implementation as a case study when there is evidence of actual work and permission to disclose it. Avoid implying that a named tool automatically guarantees security, privacy or resilience.

### C. IKAI — reflective and local-first computing

Research:
- Local data ownership, export and deletion.
- Transparent storage formats and documented schemas.
- Offline operation and graceful failure.
- Human-controlled interpretation and reflection.
- Privacy threat models and data minimisation.
- Explicit limits: no scoring, face identification, diagnosis or cloud profiling as assumed features.

Label IKAI as work in progress until a working prototype, tested behaviours and clear limitations can be demonstrated. Conceptual coherence is not implementation evidence.

### D. GENJE / GENGE ALGO — creative computation

Research livecoding, algorithmic music, audiovisual performance, choreohacking and embodied practice. Preserve provenance: performer, date, venue/context where publishable, source code or patch where shareable, licence, collaborators and recording rights. Do not treat cultural identity as decorative brand styling or claim community endorsement without consent.

### E. Knowledge base and engineering practice

Study source-first documentation, reproducible examples, versioned decisions and contributor onboarding. The knowledge base should connect:
1. Research question.
2. Source and date checked.
3. Extracted evidence.
4. Limitations / counter-evidence.
5. Interpretation.
6. Decision or recommendation.
7. Owner, review date and implementation status.

## Comparison scorecard

Score each criterion from 0–3 only after collecting evidence: **0 = absent / unknown; 1 = weak or unclear; 2 = adequate; 3 = strong and directly evidenced.** Unknown must not be treated as failure.

- Audience and problem clarity.
- Service/product scope and exclusions.
- Price and commercial transparency (where relevant).
- Mobile usability and accessibility.
- Trust and evidence quality.
- Documentation quality and onboarding.
- Privacy and data control.
- Reliability, recovery and maintainability.
- Community contribution and succession.
- Fit with NAKEBUSEN / WARENA / IKAI goals.

Include source URLs and short evidence notes beside every score. Do not collapse different domains into a single winner ranking.

## Deliverables

1. **Local competitor matrix:** verified Eldoret providers, current public services/prices, customer journey and evidence date.
2. **Website UX benchmark:** annotated observations, priority, rationale and proposed copy/design change.
3. **Technical reference catalogue:** official docs, use case, trade-offs, maturity and WARENA relevance.
4. **Creative practice bibliography:** sources, licence/attribution notes and reproducibility considerations.
5. **Decision log:** recommendation accepted/rejected/deferred, reason, owner and verification method.

## Initial recommendations

1. Prioritise the NAKEBUSEN booking journey and verifiable service information before decorative brand expansion.
2. Publish WARENA services as concrete outcomes and bounded scopes, supported by examples and handover expectations.
3. Present IKAI as research / work in progress unless a working artefact is available for inspection.
4. Make research links, source dates and uncertainty visible in internal documentation.
5. Keep English as the default for a global audience; add a Kiswahili toggle where translation is reviewed for meaning and local usage.
6. Use a feature branch and pull request for changes; do not merge this research document automatically into production or claim that a website has changed.

## Review cadence

Recheck competitor service details and prices before using them in public comparisons. Recheck software documentation when implementation decisions depend on it. Add a checked date to volatile evidence and retain a brief change log.

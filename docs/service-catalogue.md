## Publication checklist

Before publishing or changing a service entry, verify:
- Exact service name, scope and what is included.
- Current price and conditions that affect the quote.
- Materials, preparation, length, complexity and expected duration where relevant.
- Current availability and location, if stated.
- Home-visit terms and any travel cost.
- Approved contact and payment instructions.
- No invented guarantees, testimonials, qualifications or certifications.
- A review date and owner approval.

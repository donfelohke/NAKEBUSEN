## Publication checklist

Before publishing or changing a service entry, verify:
- Exact service name and scope.
- Current price and what it includes.
- Hair length, materials, preparation, or other conditions that affect the quote.
- Estimated duration and current availability, if these are to be stated.
- Location and whether home visits are offered.
- Approved contact channel and response expectations.
- Any care instructions or limitations that are actually supported.

Do not invent guarantees, testimonials, customer identities, certifications, or availability. If a detail is not confirmed, mark it as needing confirmation rather than filling it in.

## Change record

For each approved update, record the date, source of approval, changed fields, and public destinations updated. Keep private customer and transaction details out of this repository.
